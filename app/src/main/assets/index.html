A thoughtful note on gluten.

Our gluten-free ingredient filter excludes wheat ingredients. It cannot certify a meal as safe for celiac disease. Use certified gluten-free packaged ingredients (including oats, flour, pasta and spices), check labels, and use clean, separate utensils, surfaces and cooking water. Individual dietary needs should be discussed with your clinician or dietitian.
